Platform Capabilities

What AgroIntel Can Do

One intelligent platform covering everything a modern farmer needs — from soil analysis to real-time market prices.

{# 1 — Crop Recommendation #}
Crop Recommendation

Enter your soil's NPK values, temperature, humidity, and rainfall — our Random Forest model instantly tells you which crop to grow for maximum yield potential.

{# 2 — Fertilizer Advisory #}
Fertilizer Advisory

Know exactly how much Nitrogen, Phosphorus, and Potassium your soil needs. It calculates the optimal fertilizer mix tailored to your target crop and soil condition using a trained Decision Tree model.

{# 3 — Crop Price Predictor #}
Crop Price Predictor

Predict upcoming market prices, demand trajectory, and crop suitability across Indian districts using Random Forest models trained on 3-4 months of trading & historical commodity data.

{# 4 — Yield Forecast #}
Yield Forecast

Estimate total crop output in tonnes based on your land area and district-level historical production metrics — know your harvest numbers before you sow.

{# 5 — Rainfall Estimation #}
Rainfall Estimation

Auto-detect live location and view short-term rain forecasts alongside monthly rainfall predictions for every subdivision in India, derived from 114 years of IMD meteorological records.

{# 6 — Weather Forecast #}
Weather Forecast

Get 7-day weather forecasts, temperature, humidity, and wind conditions for your live location or any Indian city.

{# 7 — Live Mandi Prices #}
Live Mandi Prices

Check live government Agmarknet APMC mandi prices for major agricultural commodities, updated with fallback caching for instant market awareness.
