Platform Capabilities

What AgroIntel Can Do

One intelligent platform covering everything a modern farmer needs — from soil analysis to market rates.

{# 1 — Crop Recommendation #}
Crop Recommendation

Enter your soil's NPK values, temperature, humidity, and rainfall — our Random Forest model evaluates candidate crops and reports top recommendations with predictive probabilities.

{# 2 — Fertilizer Advisory #}
Fertilizer Advisory

Recommends targeted commercial fertilizer categories (e.g., Urea, DAP, 14-35-14) based on your soil nutrient profile and crop type using a trained Decision Tree classifier.

{# 3 — Yield Forecast #}
Yield Forecast

Estimate total harvest yield (in metric tonnes or thousand nuts for coconut) based on cultivated area and historical district production records using Random Forest regression.

{# 4 — Historical Rainfall Climatology #}
Historical Rainfall (Climatology)

Explore 115 years (1901–2015) of official IMD monthly rainfall baselines across all 36 Indian meteorological subdivisions alongside live precipitation radar.

{# 5 — Weather Forecast #}
Weather Forecast

Get 7-day weather forecasts, temperature, humidity, and wind conditions for your live location or any Indian city via Open-Meteo.

{# 6 — Mandi Prices #}
Mandi Market Intelligence

Check live government Agmarknet APMC mandi commodity rates with transparent fallback benchmark labeling when upstream servers are unreachable.
