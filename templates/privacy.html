Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Last Updated: November 2026 • In alignment with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

1. Overview & Data Fiduciary Identity

AgroIntel is an educational and academic smart agriculture platform developed to empower Indian farmers with data-driven agronomic guidance. This Privacy Policy outlines how AgroIntel acts as a Data Fiduciary, detailing the collection, processing, storage, and protection of personal data in strict compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary data required to deliver tailored agricultural insights (Data Minimisation principle):

  • Account Identifiers: Farmer Name, Email Address, and 10-digit Mobile Phone Number (for account authentication and access control).
  • Agronomic & Geographical Inputs: State, District, and field acreage (used to condition regional crop yield and mandi price queries).
  • Location Coordinates (Optional): Latitude and longitude when you explicitly click "Auto-Detect Location" or "My Location" to fetch local weather telemetry.
  • Security & Session Tokens: Cryptographically salted password hashes (bcrypt), session cookies, and HMAC CSRF protection tokens.
Data Minimisation Note: AgroIntel does not collect biometric data, Aadhaar numbers, land ownership deeds, financial bank account credentials, or unnecessary demographic markers (such as gender or date of birth).

3. Third-Party Integrations & External Telemetry

Unlike generic cloud tools that claim "zero external data transfer", AgroIntel provides complete technical transparency regarding external service requests:

  • Open-Meteo Weather API: Fetches temperature, relative humidity, and precipitation forecasts. Open-Meteo is a non-commercial open-access weather API licensed under CC BY 4.0. No user names or phone numbers are ever sent to Open-Meteo; only numerical GPS coordinates are transmitted.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim: Reverse geocodes coordinates to administrative district names under Nominatim's strict usage policy (max 1 request/second with an identifying application user-agent header).
  • Open Government Data (OGD) Agmarknet: Queries official wholesale APMC commodity arrival rates from the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare (data.gov.in). Queries are filtered strictly by public district names.
  • Local In-Process Machine Learning: Crop recommendation, fertilizer advisory, and yield forecasting algorithms run entirely in-process on the application server. Your soil metrics (N, P, K, pH) are evaluated in memory and never shared with external AI vendors.

4. Farmer Rights under DPDP Act 2023

Under Chapter III of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you hold the following statutory rights:

  • Right to Access: View all account records, profile details, and selling history stored under your farmer ID.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Request the correction of inaccurate personal data or complete deletion of your farmer account and profile records.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Submit inquiries or concerns regarding personal data handling to our designated Grievance Officer.

5. Data Retention & Security Measures

Passwords are encrypted using bcrypt with individual 12-round salt sequences. Web communications use HTTP-only, SameSite-hardened session cookies to defend against Cross-Site Scripting (XSS) and Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF). Session tokens automatically expire after inactivity.
